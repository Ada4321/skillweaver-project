What SkillWeaver does

Method

Overview of SkillWeaver. A VLM planner proposes interaction skills, which are executed by learned RL policies in simulation. A hierarchical verifier evaluates each execution and provides progress rewards and reflection, while semantic memory guides future exploration. Verified trajectories are distilled into a multimodal training dataset.

Real World Results

The real-world pipeline has two stages: large-scale simulation training (LST) on in-the-wild scenes, followed by post-training (PT) on trajectories collected from a reconstructed digital twin of the specific scene of interest. Neither stage uses a teleoperated episode. We evaluate object, colour and spatial recognition under the original setup and two distribution shifts — swapped object positions and a changed tablecloth.
